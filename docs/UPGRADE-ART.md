# Artwork credits

Rekindled's weapon illustrations are adapted from LucasArts' original artwork for
[the official Jedi Knight website](https://jkdf2.net/JKBot/JKArchive/JKDF2/JKDF2/Websites/Official%20Jedi%20Knight%20Website/Star%20Wars%20Jedi%20Knight%20Dark%20Forces%20II%20-%20Weapons_files/weapons.htm).
The original weapon images belong to LucasArts/Lucasfilm. Their web labels and
backgrounds have been removed, retaining the weapon shapes and colors.

The remaining menu and HUD assets are supplied with Rekindled. See
[the asset guide](../art/README.md) for their source files, atlas layout and processing
scripts. The original website images are retained in `art/source/official-weapons`
so the cleaned weapon atlas can be rebuilt.

Game fonts, inventory icons and fallback weapon models are loaded from the player's
own Jedi Knight installation. Original game archives and private reference captures
are not distributed with Rekindled.

The launcher uses Orbitron Medium by The League of Moveable Type, distributed under
the [SIL Open Font License](../launcher/fonts/OFL.txt).

Jedi Knight and Star Wars belong to Lucasfilm. Rekindled is an unofficial fan project.

The launcher icon is the original Jedi Knight game icon, credited to LucasArts/Lucasfilm.
