DARK FORCES II REKINDLED

An unofficial fan mod for Star Wars Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II.
Jedi Knight, Star Wars and related names and artwork belong to Lucasfilm Ltd.
Weapon illustrations are adapted from the original LucasArts Jedi Knight website.
Original game archives, sounds, models and textures are not included.

Powered by OpenJKDF2, copyright its contributors.
Engine and library license texts are supplied in the licenses folder.
The matching source archive accompanies the launcher download; see SOURCE.txt.
OpenAL is a replaceable shared library. No restriction is imposed on debugging
changes to the included LGPL components or relinking them with the engine.

Libraries and embedded components include SDL, SDL_mixer, OpenAL Soft,
GameNetworkingSockets, Protocol Buffers, PhysicsFS, GLEW, FreeGLUT, libpng,
zlib, Ogg, Vorbis, Opus, Opusfile, libsmacker, libsmusher, fcaseopen,
nativefiledialog-extended, nlohmann/json, and the MinGW/GCC runtimes.
Their respective authors retain their copyrights and license terms.

Font License 1.1. Its license is supplied as licenses/Orbitron-OFL.txt.
